# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want this project to be a welcoming place for everyone, regardless of
experience level, background, identity, or affiliation. We pledge to make
participation harassment-free and constructive.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Give technical feedback about code and behavior, not people.
- Welcome questions and different levels of experience.
- Credit the work and ideas of others.
- Respect project scope and maintainers' decisions.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, threats, or discriminatory language.
- Sexualized language or imagery.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, issues, or pull requests.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues, and pull requests
that violate this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors.

Report incidents privately to the maintainers through the repository's GitHub
security advisory form or by contacting a maintainer directly. Reports will be
reviewed promptly and kept confidential where possible.
